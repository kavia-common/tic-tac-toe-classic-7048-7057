# Shortcuts & Tips

- Mode switch:
  - Tap “Player vs Computer” for PvC (default).
  - Tap “Player vs Player” for PvP.
  - Board resets when switching modes.

- Moves:
  - Tap any empty square to place your mark.
  - In PvC, you are X; the computer is O.

- Restart:
  - Tap “Restart” to reset immediately.

- Status:
  - Shows whose turn it is, or if someone wins / draw.
  - In PvC, expect a brief “Computer thinking…” pause.

- Auto-reset:
  - The game resets after ~2 seconds when finished to keep play flowing.
